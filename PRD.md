\#\# 1\. Challenge & Problem Context

&nbsp;

\#\#\# 1.1 Executive Summary

&nbsp;

\*\*Skill Setu\*\* (\*"Skill Bridge"\*) is an AI-powered economic-mobility platform that translates years of unpaid domestic labor, caregiving, community leadership, and informal household management into verified, market-facing professional competencies.

&nbsp;

Traditional upskilling and career portals fail returning homemakers because they begin with empty resumes and resume-style drop-downs (\*"Select your skills: Python, Project Management, SEO"\*). Skill Setu flips this premise: \*\*"Don’t ask women what skills they have. Ask them what they do."\*\*

&nbsp;

By combining reflective voice/text conversational intake with a deterministic competency translation engine, an everyday time-budget profiler, micro-portfolio proof generation, and a verifiable \*\*Skill Passport\*\*, Skill Setu anchors returning women directly to viable, flexible income streams.

\#\#\# 1.2 Problem Statement

&nbsp;

Millions of women take extended breaks from formal education or the organized workforce to raise children, care for aging relatives, and run households. When attempting to re-enter income generation:

&nbsp;

1\. \*\*The Invalidation Trap:\*\* Women perceive domestic routines as having zero economic value (\*"I haven't worked in 10 years, I'm just a housewife"\*).

2\. \*\*The Blank Resume Dilemma:\*\* Job boards assume a linear corporate history. Domestic work yields no formal job titles, letters of recommendation, or bullet points.

3\. \*\*The Unrealistic Upskilling Mirage:\*\* Most skilling programs demand 40 hours/week or fixed 9-to-5 schedules, ignoring non-negotiable family care routines (school drop-offs, elder medicine schedules, cooking windows).

4\. \*\*The Impostor Barrier:\*\* Without external validation or tangible proof of work, women drop out of career re-entry pipelines before applying to their first gig or role.

\#\#\# 1.5 Core Insight & Key Differentiators

&nbsp;

\* \*\*The Paradigm Shift:\*\* Competencies are forged through execution, not just corporate employment. Running an intergenerational household of six on a fixed monthly budget with complex dietary, medical, educational, and emotional constraints is \*\*Operations & Logistics Management\*\*.

\* \*\*Key Differentiator:\*\* Skill Setu does not present a catalog of courses. It runs an \*\*interactive extraction loop\*\* that turns past action into immediate proof of work, paired with a \*\*Time-Budget Matcher\*\* that respects household realities.

\#\# 2\. The 10-Stage User Journey

&nbsp;

\`\`\`

\[1. Everyday Life\] ────► \[2. Evidence Discovery\] ────► \[3. Hidden Skill Identification\]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;│

\[6. Suitable Work Directions\] ◄─── \[5. Family/Time Constraints\] ◄───┘

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;│

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;▼

\[7. Skill Gap Analysis\] ──► \[8. First Proof of Work\] ──► \[9. 30-Day Plan\] ──► \[10. Skill Passport\]

&nbsp;

\`\`\`

&nbsp;

1\. \*\*Everyday Life:\*\* The user shares an unvarnished account of what she did this week or over the past few years (e.g., managing home renovation, tutoring neighborhood kids, organizing a medical fund, running school transport carpools).

2\. \*\*Evidence Discovery:\*\* AI acts as an empathetic biographer, asking targeted probing questions to uncover metrics, problem-solving moments, tools used, and crisis management.

3\. \*\*Hidden Skill Identification:\*\* AI maps the narrative to standardized industry frameworks (O\*NET / ESCO / National Skills Qualification Framework), extracting hard and soft competencies.

4\. \*\*Why This Skill? (Validation Loop):\*\* The system presents the extracted skill with a side-by-side justification referencing the user's specific statements, validating its market value.

5\. \*\*Family/Time Constraints:\*\* The user plots her non-negotiable household commitments across a dynamic 7-day visual weekly time-grid, producing her net available energy and hourly capacity.

6\. \*\*Suitable Work Directions:\*\* AI recommends realistic income streams (freelance, remote fractional, micro-consulting, local business operations) matching both her verified competencies and time block profiles.

7\. \*\*Skill Gap:\*\* Identifies the single missing technical tool or workflow step (e.g., knowing operations, but missing Notion or Google Sheets automation).

8\. \*\*First Proof of Work:\*\* Generates a tailored starter client brief. The user completes it within 48 hours to generate an artifact demonstrating immediate capability.

9\. \*\*30-Day Action Plan:\*\* A daily, bite-sized curriculum (45 mins/day) designed to build and package her capability without triggering household friction.

10\. \*\*Skill Passport:\*\* A public, verifiable micro-portfolio profile featuring real-world artifacts, verified competencies, availability windows, and structured references.

\#\#\# Feature 3.1: Adaptive Conversational Intake (Everyday Life & Evidence Discovery)

&nbsp;

\* \*\*WHY:\*\* Homemakers freeze when faced with blank textboxes or resume drop-downs. They require conversational prompts that focus on lived actions rather than abstract skills.

\* \*\*WHAT:\*\* A multi-turn voice/text conversational intake interface featuring prompt starters based on daily life categories:

\* \*Household Finance & Procurement:\* "Managing monthly family budgets, negotiating with contractors/vendors."

\* \*Crisis & Care Logistics:\* "Coordinating specialized schedules, elderly healthcare routines, medical regimens."

\* \*Community & Event Leadership:\* "Organizing society festivals, school fundraisers, informal chit-funds."

\* \*Education & Mentorship:\* "Tutoring children, designing study plans, behavioral coaching."

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

\* \*\*HOW AI IS USED (Genuine AI Leverage):\*\*

\* \*\*LLM Role:\*\* Conversational Interviewer utilizing an adaptive Socratic STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

\* \*\*Mechanism:\*\* Analyzes the initial user input. If the user states: \*"I just make sure my father-in-law gets his dialysis and medicines,"\* the AI detects an unquantified logistical workflow and asks: \*"That requires strict precision. How did you organize his medicine schedules, dietary charts, and hospital appointments when emergencies arose?"\*

\* \*\*Deterministic Fallback:\*\* Pre-defined clarifying branch trees if latency spikes or user provides 1-word inputs.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

\* \*\*WHAT THE USER SEES:\*\* A warm chat interface (supporting voice-to-text in local dialects and Hinglish/vernacular modes) with auto-suggestions and encouragement prompts.

\* \*\*WHAT MAKES IT MEANINGFUL:\*\* Replaces cold corporate evaluation with an empathetic listening engine that treats home routines as serious operational challenges.

&nbsp;

\#\#\# Feature 3.2: Skill Evidence Engine (Hidden Skill Identification & "Why This Skill?")

&nbsp;

\* \*\*WHY:\*\* Women need to see concrete, objective proof connecting their daily domestic activities to professional, market-standard terminology.

\* \*\*WHAT:\*\* An extraction engine that parses the conversational transcript and outputs a structured Competency Card containing:

1\. \*Extracted Professional Competency\* (e.g., "Vendor Negotiation & Cost Optimization").

2\. \*Lived Evidence Snippet\* (e.g., "Sourced materials from 5 wholesale vendors for home renovation, reducing costs by 18%").

3\. \*Industry Mapping\* (Mapped to standard roles: e.g., Junior Procurement Specialist, Operations Assistant).

4\. \*Market Value Indicator\* (Standard entry hourly rate / market demand rating).

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

\* \*\*HOW AI IS USED (Genuine AI Leverage):\*\*

\* Zero-shot extraction with Few-Shot Chain-of-Thought prompting.

\* The LLM is strictly constrained via JSON Schema output to extract competencies grounded \*exclusively\* in the user's transcript to prevent hallucinations.

\* Runs a verification pass: For each extracted skill, the model must cite the user's exact sentence as the \`grounding\_quote\`.

\#\#\# Feature 3.3: Family/Time Constraint & Reality Matcher

&nbsp;

\* \*\*WHY:\*\* Suggesting a 9-to-5 job or an inflexible 4-hour continuous block to a mother with school-aged children causes immediate churn and guilt.

\* \*\*WHAT:\*\* A daily and weekly schedule planner designed for household realities:

\* Users specify non-negotiable household blocks: School drop/pickup, cooking hours, eldercare windows, evening family routines.

\* Calculates \*\*Effective Focus Windows\*\*: Micro-pockets (e.g., 10:30 AM – 1:00 PM; 8:30 PM – 10:00 PM).

\* Categorizes work velocity: Asynchronous/Deep Work vs. Synchronous/Client-Facing.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

\* \*\*HOW AI IS USED:\*\*

\* A constraint satisfaction heuristic engine.

\* Evaluates candidate career tracks and freelance niches against the user's fragmented time blocks.

\* If a career path requires immediate, synchronous client calls during her primary caregiving windows (e.g., High-Ticket SDR / Customer Support), the algorithm drops its match score or flags it with a risk warning.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

\* \*\*WHAT THE USER SEES:\*\* A drag-and-drop or tap-to-select visual weekly time matrix showing "Family Time" (locked) and "Skill Setu Earning Windows" (green highlights), paired with a real-time \*\*Weekly Monetizable Hours\*\* indicator (e.g., \*"14 hrs/week available"\*).

\* \*\*WHAT MAKES IT MEANINGFUL:\*\* Protects the user from overcommitting and failing; honors domestic care as real work with immutable schedules.

&nbsp;

\#\#\# Feature 3.4: Opportunity Recommendation & Targeted Gap Identification

&nbsp;

\* \*\*WHY:\*\* Generic career advice ("Learn Python") is paralyzing. Users need immediate, pragmatic monetization options achievable with their existing core strengths plus one micro-upskill.

\* \*\*WHAT:\*\* Produces three targeted career tracks:

1\. \*Quickest Route to Income (Freelance / Micro-consulting):\* Leverages 90% of existing skills, requiring ≤5 days of prep.

2\. \*Sustainable Fractional Role (Part-time remote operations):\* Leverages 75% of existing skills, requiring 2-3 weeks of tool mastery.

3\. \*Growth Trajectory (Specialized client service):\* Long-term path requiring deeper capability building.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

\* Identifies the specific \*\*Tool Gap\*\* (e.g., \*"You possess Project Coordination capability, but lack familiarity with Trello/Asana"\*).

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

\* \*\*HOW AI IS USED:\*\*

\* Multi-vector semantic similarity matching between user's extracted skill vector and current remote/flexible SME gig profiles.

\* Generates a personalized \*\*Gap Analysis Matrix\*\* that separates intrinsic capability (reasoning, organization, negotiation) from superficial software tooling (Notion, Google Sheets, Canva).

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

\* \*\*WHAT THE USER SEES:\*\* 3 curated Work Cards showing realistic income potential, weekly time requirement, match score, and the exact software gap highlighted with a 2-hour learning module badge.

\* \*\*WHAT MAKES IT MEANINGFUL:\*\* Shifts the user's perspective from \*"I need a 3-year degree"\* to \*"I only need to learn this single 2-hour software tool to unlock paid work."\*

&nbsp;

\#\#\# Feature 3.5: First Proof-of-Work Generator

&nbsp;

\* \*\*WHY:\*\* Without prior professional samples, certifications are ignored by clients. Users must show, not tell.

\* \*\*WHAT:\*\* Generates a realistic, contextual simulation brief based on the user's target direction:

\* \*Example (Operations track):\* "Create a 3-day meal prep schedule, ingredient procurement inventory, and budget spreadsheet for a working couple with dietary restrictions."

\* \*Example (Social Media / Community track):\* "Draft a 1-week community engagement calendar and 3 sample posts for a local boutique bakery."

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

\* \*\*HOW AI IS USED (Genuine AI Leverage):\*\*

\* Generates the complete scenario brief, source data, client personas, and step-by-step submission template dynamically based on the user's lived context.

\* \*\*Automated Evaluation & Rubric Feedback:\*\* When the user submits her work (via document upload, structured input, or spreadsheet link), the LLM grades it against professional industry standards, providing constructive, encouraging, point-by-point feedback and actionable suggestions for improvement.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

\* \*\*WHAT THE USER SEES:\*\* An interactive \*\*Proof-of-Work Studio\*\* showing the client brief, a structured submission workspace, and an automated assessment report highlighting strengths and professional refinements.

\* \*\*WHAT MAKES IT MEANINGFUL:\*\* Produces an instant, tangible portfolio asset the user can present to clients immediately, eliminating the fear of having an empty portfolio.

&nbsp;

\#\#\# Feature 3.6: The 30-Day Micro-Action Plan

&nbsp;

\* \*\*WHY:\*\* Returning women abandon broad courses due to cognitive overload and sudden household emergencies.

\* \*\*WHAT:\*\* A personalized, modular 30-day calendar divided into 4 weekly sprints:

\* \*Week 1: Tool Mastery\* (Daily 30–45 min tasks mastering the single tool gap).

\* \*Week 2: Proof Asset Polish\* (Completing and refining 2 real-world portfolio artifacts).

\* \*Week 3: Client Pitch & Positioning\* (Writing bio, setting pricing, framing past domestic experience into professional value propositions).

\* \*Week 4: First Outreach & Applications\* (Submitting to 3 vetted, time-compatible flexible gigs or local small businesses).

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

\* \*\*HOW AI IS USED:\*\*

\* Dynamic schedule adaptation: If the user marks a day as \*"Family Emergency / Child Sick"\*, the system recalibrates remaining sprint milestones without guilt or penalty, reallocating tasks into future open slots.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

\* \*\*WHAT THE USER SEES:\*\* A gamified, calm checklist calendar showing only today's 30-minute task with direct launch buttons.

\* \*\*WHAT MAKES IT MEANINGFUL:\*\* Turns an intimidating career transition into small, manageable daily steps that fit comfortably alongside household routines.

&nbsp;

\#\#\# Feature 3.7: The Verified Skill Passport

&nbsp;

\* \*\*WHY:\*\* Resumes highlight employment gaps; modern hiring requires a dynamic, skills-first, evidence-backed digital credential.

\* \*\*WHAT:\*\* A public-facing web profile (\`skillsetu.work/p/username\`) containing:

1\. Professional identity statement (free of apologies for caregiving gaps).

2\. Verified functional competencies with direct links to the user's completed Proofs of Work.

3\. Time availability badge (e.g., \*"Available for 15 hrs/week • Mon-Fri Mornings"\*).

4\. Audio intro: A 60-second guided audio introduction of the candidate speaking about her work focus.

5\. Downloadable 1-page modern Skill-First Resume (PDF).

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

\* \*\*HOW AI IS USED:\*\*

\* Compiles all upstream outputs (intake transcripts, evidence quotes, completed proof of work, tool proficiencies) into a clean, professionally phrased portfolio and standard JSON-LD structured resume schema.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

\* \*\*WHAT THE USER SEES:\*\* A mobile-responsive web portfolio preview with an instant share link, QR code, and a "Download Clean Resume" button.

\* \*\*WHAT MAKES IT MEANINGFUL:\*\* Converts a 10-year resume gap into a compelling showcase of practical capability and immediate availability.

\#\# 5\. Trust, Safety & Ethics

&nbsp;

\`\`\`

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

│                        TRUST & SAFETY FRAMEWORK                        │

├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤

│ • Zero Psychological Gaslighting (Strictly forbid hallucinated skills) │

│ • Explicit Safeguarding of Children & Family Privacy (PII Scrubbing)  │

│ • Scam Opportunity Shielding (Only legitimate, non-MLM work models)    │

│ • Dignified, Respectful Tone Across All Vernacular Outputs            │

└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

&nbsp;

\`\`\`

&nbsp;

1\. \*\*Family Privacy & PII Scrubbing:\*\* Users frequently mention sensitive family details (e.g., children's names, schools, specific medical diagnoses of elders). The intake pipeline runs a regex and entity-scrubbing filter before storing transcripts or feeding them to downstream prompts.

2\. \*\*Zero False Promises (Anti-Hallucination):\*\* The system must never tell a user she is ready for a specialized technical role (e.g., Financial Auditor) without genuine technical credentials. All translated skills must map honestly to entry/fractional market tasks (e.g., Bookkeeping Assistant, Logistics Coordinator).

3\. \*\*Anti-Exploitation & Scam Guardrails:\*\* Recommended work directions must exclude multi-level marketing (MLM), data-entry scams, or predatory fee-charging job boards. The system restricts recommendations to verifiable direct freelancing and SME support tracks.

4\. \*\*Tone & Empathy Guardrails:\*\* Prompts must balance encouragement with pragmatism. Avoid condescension (\*"Good job for a housewife\!"\*). Treat caregiving routines with professional dignity, recognizing them as real-world operational execution.
